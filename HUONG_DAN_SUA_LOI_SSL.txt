BAN VA LOI KHONG GUI DUOC KET QUA IN VE TELEGRAM
=================================================

NGUYEN NHAN
- PDFtoPrinter.exe in xong, returncode = 0: khong phai loi may in.
- start.py cu gui sendMessage bang urllib va bi SSL CERTIFICATE_VERIFY_FAILED.
- Nhan tin Telegram (getUpdates) van HTTP 200 vi dung thu vien python-telegram-bot.

DA SUA
1. start.py: gui trang thai bang python-telegram-bot (su dung HTTPX, xac thuc SSL).
2. Luu ket qua in vao thu muc "telegram_pending_status" truoc khi gui.
3. Neu mang/Telegram loi: gui lai tu dong sau 5, 10, 20, 40... toi da 300 giay.
4. Khoi dong lai chuong trinh khong lam mat cac ket qua dang cho gui.
5. Neu co nhieu chat, chi gui lai cho chat chua nhan duoc thong bao.
6. telegram_listener.py: tat log INFO HTTPX co chua BOT_TOKEN trong URL.
7. Khong thay doi print_engine.py, cach in, giay, may in hay bo cuc tem.
8. KHONG vo hieu hoa xac minh chung chi SSL.

CACH CAP NHAT TREN WINDOWS
1. Dung cua so run_all.bat cu (Ctrl+C, hoac dong cua so).
2. Sao luu 2 file cu: C:\chrome-auto-print\start.py va
   C:\chrome-auto-print\telegram_listener.py.
3. Giai nen goi BAN_VA va chep de 2 file start.py va
   telegram_listener.py vao C:\chrome-auto-print\.
4. GIU NGUYEN bot_secrets.py, print_engine.py, print_config_loader.py,
   tools\PDFtoPrinter.exe, printing\print_settings.config, dev_profile.
5. Chay lai C:\chrome-auto-print\run_all.bat.
   Neu bao thieu module telegram: chay install_requirements.bat truoc.
6. Gui mot file test vao bot; kiem tra:
   [TELEGRAM] Print result saved for delivery: ...
   [TELEGRAM] Sent print result to chat <id>
   Bot phan hoi: "PDFtoPrinter.exe: THANH CONG".

KHI MAT MANG
- Cac file *.json trong "telegram_pending_status" la thong bao chua gui.
- Chung se tu dong duoc gui lai khi mang hoat dong.
- Khong xoa thu muc nay neu con thong bao muon nhan.
- Neu phan mem bi buoc dong ngay sau khi Telegram nhan thong bao
  nhung truoc khi xoa JSON, co the nhan trung thong bao sau khi mo lai.

NEU VAN GAP LOI SSL
- Kiem tra gio Windows va proxy/antivirus chan SSL (HTTPS inspection).
- Bao dam may tin cay chung chi goc hop le cua proxy noi bo neu co.
- Khong su dung verify=False / bo qua chung chi, vi de lo bot token.
- Neu log "getUpdates" chay nhung gui bao loi, kiem tra firewall/proxy
  cho phep Telegram Bot API sendMessage.

BAO MAT
- Token bot nam trong file bot_secrets.py CUA BAN, khong duoc chep vao goi va.
- Anh log cu co hien duong dan Telegram kem token. Neu da chia se anh hoac
  file ZIP ra ngoai, nen thu hoi token cu trong @BotFather (/revoke),
  cap nhat token moi vao bot_secrets.py va khoi dong lai.

Luu y: Ban va da duoc kiem tra cu phap va thu nghiem mo phong luong gui
thanh cong, mat mang, gui mot phan va thu lai. Chua thu tren may in va
ket noi Telegram thuc te cua ban.
